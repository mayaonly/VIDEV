01 / OUR STORY
How it started

From one editing bay to a full production studio.

VIDEV MEDIA started in 2019 as a single freelance editor working out of a bedroom with a mid-range laptop and a stubborn belief that YouTube editing could be faster, sharper and less painful than what most creators were settling for.

Six years later we're a full-service YouTube production agency — editing, scripting, packaging, Shorts systems, full shoots and channel management — supporting 120+ creators across commentary, documentary, podcast, review and tech.

We're still small on purpose. Every channel we take on gets a real team behind it, not a queue number.

04 / VALUES
How we work

Four rules we don't break.

Everything about how VIDEV MEDIA runs comes down to these.

01

Ship on time.

If we say Friday, it's Friday. Deadlines aren't a target, they're the baseline for the whole relationship.

02

No black boxes.

You see every draft, every comment, every decision in your portal. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

03

Retention over polish.

A beautiful edit that loses viewers is a failure. Every cut, sound and beat is judged on whether it holds attention.

04

Treat channels like ours.

We only take on work we'd be proud to run on our own channel. If we don't believe in the fit, we'll say so.
